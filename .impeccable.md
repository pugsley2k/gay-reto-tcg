## Design Context

### Users
Pokémon card collectors browsing and buying singles, often on phones; the owner scans and reviews stock from a phone.

### Brand Personality
Retro, personal, straightforward. Avoid invented slogans and artificial hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-approved charcoal storefront, cream script logo with cream (#FAEAC4) accents, large artwork carousel, real listing ribbon.

### Design Principles
Keep card artwork prominent. Make purchasing details legible. Preserve accurate stock and explicit seller review. Use purposeful motion with pause and reduced-motion support. Keep public pages consistent.
