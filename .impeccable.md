## Design context

Gay Retro TCG is an independent Pokémon singles shop for collectors browsing on phones and desktops. The creator rejected the restrained cream editorial redesign and asked for a distinctive identity and animation, while trusting design decisions to the agent.

Brand voice: unapologetic, nostalgic, playful. Direction: collector's club meets retro arcade poster; charcoal ink, acid yellow, muted candy pink, condensed display typography, card artwork as the primary material. Avoid generic startup layouts, glowing neon templates, beige lifestyle-store styling and invented stock claims.

Principles: Cards are the stars. Pride is expressed with confident identity rather than decoration everywhere. Interactive motion should feel physical and respond to the user. The store remains readable and usable on touch devices. Respect reduced motion. Preserve the seller scanner and commerce behaviour.
