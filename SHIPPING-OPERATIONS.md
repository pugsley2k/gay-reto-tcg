# Delivery setup — 14 October 2026

UK delivery only; dispatch within 3–5 working days. Customer contact: gayretrotcg@gmail.com.

Customer charges include postage and packing. For orders up to £75: Tracked 48 £4.50; Tracked 24 £5.75; optional Special Delivery from £11.95. Above £75, Special Delivery is mandatory. Above £2,500, online checkout asks the customer to email for a delivery arrangement.

Special Delivery bands follow card subtotal: up to £750, up to £1,000, up to £2,500. Customer rates are online postage plus £1.10 packing. Weight allowance is 100g packaging + 15g per sleeved single, rounded up to the 500g/1kg/2kg postal band. This is an estimate for singles, not a label-buying integration. Weigh every packed order; update the allowance/rates if the actual packaging requires it. Graded slabs or sealed products need separate shipping rules before being sold here.

The selected service, charge, compensation band, subtotal and total are saved with each new order. The seller order log shows these fields. Delivery address remains in PayPal. Buy the corresponding service and compensation level when posting, retain proof of posting and purchase/value evidence, and send the customer their tracking reference. Labels and tracking emails are not purchased or sent automatically.

Royal Mail compensation is subject to its exclusions, evidence requirements and actual-loss assessment; a displayed band is not a guarantee of a successful claim. Do not confuse consequential-loss cover with extra cover for card value. Customer statutory rights apply regardless of the carrier's claim decision.
